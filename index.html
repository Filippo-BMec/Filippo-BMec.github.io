Regional, Industrial and Defense Economics

Filippo Berti Mecocci

Postdoctoral Researcher, University of Parma
PhD in Development Economics and Local Systems (DELoS), University of Florence

I study how market structures and local production systems shape productivity, employment, and spatial economic outcomes. My recent work examines the aggregate and local effects of Europe's new arms race under the EU Readiness 2030 framework.
